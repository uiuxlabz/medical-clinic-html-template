What We Offer

Comprehensive medical services under one roof

Prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up — coordinated across every department.

Emergency Care

24/7 urgent care for chest pain, breathing difficulty, injuries, and sudden illness — with rapid triage.

Cardiology

Heart health screening, ECG, stress testing, blood-pressure management, and cardiac rehabilitation.

Pediatrics

Well-child visits, vaccinations, sick care, and developmental screenings for kids of every age.

Diagnostics & Lab

On-site blood work, imaging, and pathology with in-house analysis and same-day results.

Pharmacy

Convenient on-site pharmacy for prescriptions, refills, and personalised medication counselling.

Gynaecology, prenatal care, mammography, and wellness screenings in a private, comfortable setting.
