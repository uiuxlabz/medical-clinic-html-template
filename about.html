Discover our story, our team, and the values that drive everything we do at Medinova Medical Clinic.

Our Story

25 Years of Trusted Healthcare

Founded in 2001, Medinova Medical Clinic began with a simple mission: to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare to our community. What started as a small family practice has grown into a comprehensive medical center serving thousands of patients each year.

Our founders, Dr. Robert and Dr. Amanda Hayes, believed that healthcare should be both medically excellent and deeply personal. That philosophy continues to guide every aspect of our practice today.

Over the decades, we have expanded our facilities, invested in cutting-edge technology, and recruited some of the finest medical professionals in the region — all while maintaining the warm, patient-first approach that defines Medinova.
