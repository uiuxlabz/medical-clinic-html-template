Our Departments

Comprehensive medical services delivered by specialized teams using the latest technology and evidence-based practices.

♥

Cardiology

Our cardiology department provides comprehensive heart care from preventive screenings to advanced interventional procedures. Our team of board-certified cardiologists uses the latest diagnostic and treatment technologies to manage conditions ranging from hypertension to complex heart disease.

  • Cardiac catheterization and angioplasty
  • Echocardiography and stress testing
  • Heart failure management
  • Arrhythmia monitoring and treatment
  • Preventive cardiac screenings
  • Cardiac rehabilitation programs
☤

Neurology

Our neurology department specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the nervous system. From migraines and epilepsy to stroke and neurodegenerative diseases, our neurologists combine clinical expertise with advanced neuroimaging and diagnostic tools.

  • Stroke diagnosis and emergency treatment
  • Epilepsy monitoring and management
  • Multiple sclerosis care
  • Headache and migraine clinics
  • Neurophysiology and EMG testing
  • Cognitive and memory assessments
☯

Orthopedics

Our orthopedic department offers expert care for conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, and ligaments. Our surgeons perform both minimally invasive and traditional procedures, supported by comprehensive rehabilitation services.

  • Joint replacement surgery (knee, hip, shoulder)
  • Arthroscopic surgery
  • Sports medicine and injury treatment
  • Spinal surgery and back care
  • Fracture treatment and trauma care
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation
☩

Pediatrics

Our pediatrics department provides warm, child-friendly medical care from birth through adolescence. We focus on preventive health, developmental milestones, and creating a comfortable experience for both children and their families.

  • Newborn and infant wellness visits
  • Immunization and vaccination programs
  • Growth and developmental assessments
  • Pediatric illness and infection treatment
  • Allergy and asthma management
  • Adolescent health services
☥

Ophthalmology

Complete eye care services from routine vision exams to advanced surgical procedures. Our ophthalmologists use cutting-edge technology to diagnose and treat a full spectrum of eye conditions and vision disorders.

  • Comprehensive eye examinations
  • Cataract and glaucoma surgery
  • Retinal disease diagnosis and treatment
  • Laser vision correction (LASIK)
  • Pediatric ophthalmology
  • Dry eye and corneal care
✚

General Surgery

Our general surgery department performs a wide range of procedures using both minimally invasive and traditional surgical techniques. Our experienced surgeons work in modern, fully equipped operating rooms for optimal patient outcomes.

  • Laparoscopic and minimally invasive surgery
  • Hernia repair and abdominal surgery
  • Appendectomy and gallbladder removal
  • Thyroid and endocrine surgery
  • Breast surgery and biopsy
  • Pre and post-operative care programs
